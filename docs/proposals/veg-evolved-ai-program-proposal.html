02 · Why

Why this matters

Help people stay in the profession longer.

Emergency medicine asks more of our people than almost any corner of vet med: overnights, compassion fatigue, decision load, and the administrative weight that follows everyone home.

  • Burnout and attrition are the profession's defining retention problem — and ER shifts concentrate both.
  • Mental-health risk in veterinary medicine is well documented; support that meets people where they already are gets used, support that requires another app or portal doesn't.
  • The cost of losing one trained ER doctor or nurse — recruiting, credentialing, ramp-up — dwarfs the cost of a wellness program for an entire hospital.

The goal is simple: reduce the daily load that drives burnout, so veggies stay healthier, happier, and at VEG longer. Even if it only helps one, it's worth doing — but it's built to help many.

03 · The Program

What the program is

A personal AI companion that lives in WhatsApp.

No new app, no portal, no login friction. Each veggie gets their own private AI assistant that learns them over time and handles the daily load.

Daily load

Calendar management, daily briefings, task capture, reminders, email triage and drafted replies.

Wellness

Energy and wellness check-ins, decompression support after hard shifts, boundary scripting for saying no without guilt.

Grows with you

Learns each person's life and builds for them over time — personal dashboards, shift trackers, weekly reviews.

Retail, this product sells to the public at $49/month. VEG would never pay retail — this proposal is cost-basis only, per our conversation.

04 · Under the Hood

How it works

One private AI per person — not a shared chatbot.

Isolated & personal

Every veggie gets their own dedicated AI workspace. Their assistant's memory, personality, and files belong to them alone.

Already built & running

The platform is live today with active subscribers: WhatsApp messaging, voice notes, memory, and personal page-building all work now.

One click via Okta

The website lives as a tile in VEG's Okta dashboard. A veggie clicks it and lands in their assistant — no new passwords, no sign-up friction.

Zero IT burden on VEG

Hosting, security, provisioning, and support are all handled by the program. VEG's only role is sponsorship and adding the tile.

05 · The Model

The sponsorship model

Same playbook as the large-animal training partnership.

Exactly like the farm-training program with Rachel and Megan's family: a veggie-owned initiative, run independently, that VEG sponsors at cost because it benefits veggies.

  • Bethany owns and operates the program. It is an independent initiative, not a VEG product.
  • VEG sponsors the cost, not the price. VEG pays actual expenses to run the program for its people — no margin, no markup.
  • Veggies opt in. Membership is voluntary and open to every veggie — one flat sponsorship covers them all.
  • Outside sales stay separate. The public-facing product continues independently and will never present itself as VEG-sponsored or VEG-branded.

08 · Okta Access

Access: one tile in Okta

Veggies find it where they find everything else.

The Evolved AI website becomes a tile in VEG's Okta dashboard. Single sign-on is already built into the platform — this costs VEG nothing and takes IT minutes, not months.

Already built

Okta single sign-on (OIDC) is live in the platform today. No custom development, no waiting on a roadmap.

What VEG IT does

Add the app tile in Okta and assign it to veggies. Standard OIDC app — client ID, secret, redirect URL. About 15 minutes.

What a veggie does

Clicks the tile. Their account is created on first click and they land in their assistant — no passwords, no sign-up form, nothing to remember.

One-time setup cost to VEG: $0. Platform development is already complete and sunk — VEG is never asked to fund product development, only the running costs.

09 · Privacy

Privacy & boundaries

What a veggie tells their AI stays theirs.

Private by design

Conversations, memories, and personal files live in each veggie's isolated workspace. Nothing is shared between users — or with VEG.

What VEG sees

Aggregate, anonymized program metrics only: enrollment, active usage, and opt-in satisfaction surveys. Never message content.

Not an employer tool

The AI works for the veggie, not for management. That trust is what makes people actually use it — and what makes it work.

Clean separation

The independent, public version of the product carries no VEG branding or sponsorship claims, protecting both sides.
