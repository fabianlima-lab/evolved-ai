02 · Why

Why this matters

Help people stay in the profession longer.

Emergency medicine asks more of our people than almost any corner of vet med: overnights, compassion fatigue, decision load, and the administrative weight that follows everyone home.

  • Burnout and attrition are the profession's defining retention problem — and ER shifts concentrate both.
  • Mental-health risk in veterinary medicine is well documented; support built by someone who understands the work gets used — generic corporate wellness programs don’t.
  • The cost of losing one trained ER doctor or nurse — recruiting, credentialing, ramp-up — dwarfs the cost of a wellness program for an entire hospital.

The goal is simple: reduce the daily load that drives burnout, so veggies stay healthier, happier, and at VEG longer. Even if it only helps one, it's worth doing — but it's built to help many.

05 · The Model

The sponsorship model

Same playbook as the large-animal training partnership.

Exactly like the farm-training program with Rachel and Megan's family: a veggie-owned initiative, run independently, that VEG sponsors at cost because it benefits veggies.

  • Bethany owns and operates the program. It is an independent initiative, not a VEG product.
  • VEG sponsors the cost, not the price. VEG pays actual expenses to run the program for its people — no margin, no markup.
  • Veggies opt in. Membership is voluntary and open to every veggie — one flat sponsorship covers them all.
  • Outside sales stay separate. The public-facing product continues independently and will never present itself as VEG-sponsored or VEG-branded.
  • Built to outlast circumstances. If Bethany ever parts from VEG, the contract is simply reevaluated at that time — no disruption for enrolled veggies.

09 · Privacy

Privacy & boundaries

What a veggie shares inside stays theirs.

Private by design

What veggies share in coaching calls and the TEV Connect community stays inside the membership. VEG never receives names, session content, or anything a member says.

What VEG sees

Aggregate, anonymized program metrics only: enrollment, active usage, and opt-in satisfaction surveys.

Not an employer tool

The membership works for the veggie, not for management. That trust is what makes people actually show up — and what makes it work.

Clean separation

The independent, public membership carries no VEG branding or sponsorship claims, protecting both sides.
