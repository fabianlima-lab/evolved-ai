## 1. Summary

Following our conversation: VEG doesn't buy the product — VEG **sponsors the cost** of running the program for veggies who opt in, exactly like the large-animal training partnership (Rachel Stauffer / Megan Figger's family farms). Bethany owns and operates the program independently. The public version of the product continues separately and will never present itself as VEG-branded or VEG-sponsored.

- **Retail price of the product:** $49/month
- **VEG's cost basis:** ~$11.50–$16.70 per enrolled veggie/month depending on cohort size, plus a one-time setup
- **Proposed start:** 90-day pilot, 25 veggies, opt-in

## 2. What veggies get

A personal AI companion that lives in WhatsApp (no new app or portal). Each veggie gets their own private, isolated AI that learns them over time:

- Calendar management, daily briefings, task capture, reminders
- Email triage and drafted replies
- Wellness & energy check-ins, post-shift decompression, boundary scripting
- Personal dashboards, shift trackers, and weekly reviews the AI builds for them
- Voice notes supported (hands-free)

The platform is live today with active paying subscribers — VEG is not funding product development.

**Pilot all-in estimate:** ~$3,750 setup + ~$420/mo × 3 months ≈ **$5,000 for the full 90-day pilot.**

## 4. Privacy & boundaries

- Each veggie's conversations, memories, and files live in their own isolated workspace — never shared with other users or with VEG.
- VEG receives aggregate, anonymized program metrics only: enrollment, active usage, opt-in satisfaction surveys. Never message content.
- The AI works for the veggie, not for management — that trust is what drives adoption.
- The independent public product carries no VEG branding or sponsorship claims.

## 5. Pilot proposal

- **Scope:** 25 veggies, opt-in, 90 days (one hospital or first 25 volunteers network-wide)
- **Metrics:** weekly active usage, self-reported stress/workload pulse, retention intent, NPS
- **Decision point:** at day 90, review data together — expand, adjust, or wind down cleanly. No long-term commitment.
