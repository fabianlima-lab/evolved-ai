## 1. The offer

One thing, kept simple: **veggies get access to the existing Evolved AI membership.** VEG sponsors the program at cost — a flat monthly amount that covers TEV's documented operating expenses — and membership opens to every veggie who wants it. Same structure as the large-animal training partnership: an independent program owned and run by Bethany, sponsored by VEG because it benefits veggies. The public version stays fully separate and never carries VEG branding.

**Access is one click:** the Evolved AI website becomes a tile in VEG's Okta dashboard. A veggie clicks the tile, their account is created automatically, and they land in their assistant. No passwords, no sign-up forms. (Okta single sign-on is already built into the platform — setup cost to VEG is $0.)

## 2. What the membership is

A personal AI companion that lives in WhatsApp (plus the web dashboard). Each member gets their own private, isolated AI that learns them over time:

- Calendar management, daily briefings, task capture, reminders
- Email triage and drafted replies
- Wellness & energy check-ins, post-shift decompression, boundary scripting
- Personal dashboards, shift trackers, and weekly reviews the AI builds for them
- Voice notes supported (hands-free)

Retail price to the public: **$49/month per member.**

## 4. The ask

- **Flat monthly sponsorship: $2,500/month** (proposed; trued up to receipts once the remaining lines are confirmed).
- **Unlimited veggie enrollment** — no per-seat fees, no cap. The sponsorship never grows with enrollment.
- Break-even against retail is **51 enrolled veggies**; at 100 enrolled the effective cost is **$25/veggie/month** (49% below retail), and it keeps falling as more join.
- **90-day initial term** (~$7,500 total commitment), then review enrollment and actual costs together: continue, adjust, or wind down cleanly.

## 5. Privacy & boundaries

- Each veggie's conversations, memories, and files live in their own isolated workspace — never shared with other users or with VEG.
- VEG receives aggregate, anonymized program metrics only: enrollment, active usage, opt-in satisfaction surveys. Never message content.
- The AI works for the veggie, not for management — that trust is what drives adoption.
- The independent public product carries no VEG branding or sponsorship claims.
